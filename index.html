By measuring resilience in four dimensions – individual, household, community and societal,
the Index provides the most holistic assessment of resilience available at a global level.
The 2026 Resilience Index is built using blended data from the World Risk Poll,
collected in 2025. It updates the previous Index published in 2022 and 2024,
using the same methodology and with data collected in 2021 and 2023 respectively.

Individual Level:
If a disaster were to occur near you in the future, do you think there is anything you could do to protect yourself or your family from its impact?

Household Level:
Suppose your household suddenly lost all income and had to survive only on savings and things that could be sold. How long would your household be able to cover all the basic needs, such as food, housing, and transportation?

Community Level:
How much do you think most of your neighbours care about you and your wellbeing?
Do you feel safe walking alone at night in the city or area where you live?
Have you done any of the following in the past month? Helped a stranger or someone you didn’t know who needed help.

Societal Level:
How much do you think the government of [country] cares about you and your wellbeing?
Do you have confidence in each of the following, or not?
The military?
The judicial system or courts?
The national government?
